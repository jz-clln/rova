# MVP Workflow

## Buyer demand first
A buyer creates a requirement with commodity, kilograms, product specifications, receiving destination, receiving window, and deadline.

## Supply confirmation
Farmers can forecast supply, then confirm actual harvested quantity.

## Allocation
Rova selects compatible confirmed supply until the requirement is filled or compatible supply runs out.

## Route feasibility
Before confirming a route, check truck capacity, pickup or collection-point geography, route detour, loading windows, receiving window, commodity compatibility, expected arrival, and estimated route economics.

## Direct B2B delivery
The initial model sends one consolidated load to one buyer or market destination.

## Receiver confirmation
The route closes only after the buyer records receipt, actual quantity, time, and condition notes.
