# Rova Gaps and Controls

## Route density
Risk: many users still produce few compatible loads.
Control: launch corridor by corridor around an anchor buyer and recurring schedule.

## Off-platform leakage
Risk: farmer and driver exchange numbers.
Control: monetize coordination, not introductions. Keep pooling, route building, replacement loads, records, recurring routes, and receiver confirmation valuable inside Rova.

## Farm pickup inefficiency
Risk: remote farms destroy route savings.
Control: support collection points and cooperative aggregation. Direct farm pickup only when economics allow it.

## Harvest uncertainty
Risk: expected 500 kg becomes 360 kg.
Control: forecast -> confirmed supply, cutoff times, and replacement-supply matching.

## Buyer rejection
Risk: produce is rejected at destination.
Control: product specifications, future photos, grade / variety metadata, pickup quantity, receiver confirmation, and a dispute trail.

## Receiving deadline
Risk: a full truck arrives too late.
Control: receiving windows are hard route constraints.

## Detour economics
Risk: a small load adds a large detour.
Control: route contribution check before accepting it.

## Cancellations
Risk: route becomes uneconomic.
Control: backup supply and replacement matching.

## Driver waiting time
Risk: too much idle time at pickups.
Control: pickup windows, readiness confirmation, collection points, and later waiting-time rules.

## Cargo compatibility
Risk: products require different handling.
Control: commodity compatibility and cold-chain attributes.

## Three-sided liquidity
Risk: farmers, carriers, and buyers wait for each other.
Control: anchor buyer first, then seed supply and carrier capacity manually.

## Unit economics
Risk: Rova creates savings but loses money per route.
Control: measure route contribution margin from pilot day one.
