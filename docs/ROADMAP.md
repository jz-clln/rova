# Roadmap

## Phase 1 - Prove pooling
Buyer requirement, farm supply, deterministic matching, route creation, carrier assignment, pickup confirmation, direct buyer delivery, receiver confirmation, manual admin intervention.

## Phase 2 - Make it reliable
Notifications, cancellation handling, backup supply, carrier verification, proof of pickup / delivery, receiving windows, disputes.

## Phase 3 - Make Rova hard to leave
Recurring routes, automatic replacement capacity, backhaul matching, cooperative dashboard, buyer operations dashboard, regulated payment integration.

## Phase 4 - Optimize the network
Road-network route engine, OR-Tools service, multi-drop feasibility, contribution margin, demand forecasting, richer commodity constraints.

## Phase 5 - Expand corridor by corridor
Only enter a new corridor after buyer demand, supply, carrier capacity, and receiving destinations are dense enough.
