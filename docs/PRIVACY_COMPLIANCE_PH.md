# Philippine Privacy and Compliance Checklist

Engineering planning only. It is not legal advice. Before commercial launch, use Philippine counsel and an accountant for the final operating model.

## Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)
Design around transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality, security, and data-subject rights.

Engineering actions:
- privacy impact assessment before pilot
- data inventory and purpose mapping
- role-based access
- retention schedule
- data-subject request process
- vendor / processor review
- breach response plan
- DPO responsibility and NPC registration assessment

## Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967)
Relevant if Rova evolves into an e-marketplace. Plan for merchant verification, accurate listing information, redress workflows, seller records, and data protection.

## Electronic Commerce Act (RA 8792)
Keep verifiable electronic records for confirmations. Store actor and timestamp history rather than only overwriting status fields.

## Food Safety Act (RA 10611)
If Rova later handles physical consolidation facilities, storage, cold chain, or becomes a food business operator, obligations increase. Preserve traceability from farm supply to receiving buyer.

## BIR / e-commerce
If Rova processes marketplace transactions or remits seller proceeds, get accounting advice before launch. Registration, invoicing, and withholding rules may apply depending on the model.

## Payments / BSP
Do not create an informal Rova wallet. Use appropriately regulated payment providers if Rova later collects or splits funds.

## Truck-for-hire / LTFRB
Verify participating carriers have the authorization required for commercial transport. Transport counsel should confirm whether Rova remains software / coordination or takes on a regulated transportation role.

## Documents to prepare before a real pilot
Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Carrier Agreement, Buyer Agreement, Farmer / Supplier Agreement, Cancellation Policy, Delivery and Proof-of-Receipt Policy, Produce Quality Policy if procurement is enabled, Dispute Process, Data Retention Policy, Incident Response Plan.
