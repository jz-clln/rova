# Rova Product Scope

## Product definition
Rova is an agricultural freight coordination platform that aggregates buyer demand, matches supply from multiple farms, consolidates compatible shipments, and organizes shared delivery routes to buyers and markets.

## Core wedge
**MULTI-FARM -> ONE LOAD -> ONE BUYER**

Later: **MULTI-FARM -> MULTI-LOAD -> MULTI-BUYER OPTIMIZATION**

## MVP workflow
1. Anchor buyer creates a produce requirement.
2. Farmers submit or confirm available quantity.
3. Rova allocates compatible supply to the requirement.
4. Rova checks pickup geography, capacity, timing, commodity compatibility, and receiving deadline.
5. Rova creates one shared route.
6. Authorized truck operator / driver accepts or is assigned.
7. Pickups are confirmed.
8. Consolidated goods are delivered directly to the buyer or market.
9. Buyer confirms receipt and quantity.

## Delivery scope
Included: direct B2B delivery to the buyer's specified receiving location.
Not initially included: consumer last-mile door-to-door delivery after the buyer receives the goods.

## Pickup scope
Prefer collection points / cooperative aggregation when direct farm pickup creates too much detour or waiting time. Farm pickup can still be supported when accessible and economical.

## Launch shape
- 1 anchor buyer
- 1 recurring destination
- 1 farmer cluster or cooperative
- 10 to 30 farmers
- 2 to 3 truck operators
- 1 to 2 product categories
- 1 recurring delivery schedule

## Not in MVP
Nationwide launch, open consumer marketplace, Rova-owned fleet, Rova-owned warehouse network, Rova wallet, consumer delivery, complex multi-drop routing, AI forecasting, dynamic pricing, advanced financing.
